UK ECOMMERCE SALES ANALYSIS DASHBOARD — 2026 YTD

📊 Built a UK E-commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard in Excel — using the STAR approach

I recently completed an interactive UK E-commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard in Excel, designed to turn raw sales data into clear business insights.

S — Situation
The dataset contained sales, profit, customer, order, return, product, category, channel, payment method and regional data. The challenge was to make all of this information easy to understand from a single management view.

T — Task
My goal was to create an interactive dashboard that would help decision-makers quickly answer questions such as:

• How are sales and profit performing?
• Are customers and orders growing or declining?
• Which categories and products are performing best?
• Which sales channels are contributing the most revenue?
• How is the return rate changing?
• Which UK regions are generating the highest sales?

A — Action
Using Excel, I built a formula-driven dashboard with:

📈 Sales and Profit trend analysis
💰 Sales, Profit and Profit Margin KPIs
📦 Quantity and Order metrics
👥 Unique Customer analysis
↩️ Return Rate and Completed Order tracking
🏆 Top Products and Top Regions
🛍️ Category-wise Sales Share and Profit contribution
📣 Sales Channel analysis
💳 Payment Method analysis
🎛️ Dynamic filters for Year, Region and Category
📊 YoY and YTD calculations
💡 A dynamic Key Insight section for executive-level interpretation

I also focused on clean presentation, consistent KPI formatting, conditional colours, and making the yellow cells the only user inputs while the rest of the dashboard updates automatically.

R — Result
The final 2026 YTD dashboard highlights:

💷 £293.7K Sales
💰 £69.3K Profit
📦 5.2K Units Sold
🧾 2.5K Orders
👥 2.0K Unique Customers
📊 23.59% Profit Margin
↩️ 9.1% Return Rate

The dashboard also reveals an important business story:

Sales are down 29.15% YoY, mainly due to lower customer and order volumes. However, AOV increased 5.11%, the Return Rate improved by 0.55 percentage points, and the Profit Margin remained stable at 23.59%.

That’s the part I enjoy most about dashboard development: The goal is not just to show numbers — it is to make the business story easier to understand and act on.
